.. _tutorials:

Tutorials
=========

The tutorials below present Neper's capabilities, one topic at a time, with the modules they use given in parentheses.  Each page shows the commands, step by step, and the images they produce (made by the :ref:`neper_v`, with POV-Ray).  Most pages give, at their beginning, the settings that reproduce their images exactly, and some pages provide their input files (simulation directories, orientation files, scripts, etc.) for download.

Commands are run in a terminal, as described in :ref:`introduction` (see also :ref:`python_interface` for running Neper from Python).
